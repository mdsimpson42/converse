As part of the ConveRSE project, we want to create a number of resources that can be used by the community to support mental health in the workplace.

For example, we hope to create:

  • Posters that you can display in your workplace
  • Leaflets with useful information for your team/colleagues
  • Downloadable documents
  • Example templates for policies and procedures

Leaflet

We have created a leaflet, which Mike has been handing out whenever he gives his talk. A new version was created in August 2026.

It is a double-sided, A5 leaflet, containing some mental health statistics, what to do if you are struggling, some charities that support mental health, information about the ConveRSE project and how to get involved.

  • You can download a PDF version of the leaflet, if you'd like to print copies to hand out to your colleagues.

Poster

In 2026, we created a poster, which will be available for the first time at RSECon26 and is available for download below.

It is an A4 poster, that encourages people to be more open about mental health in the workplace, plus suggestions on what to do if you are struggling and a link to the ConveRSE website.

  • You can download a PDF version of the poster, if you'd like to print it and put it up in your workplace, i.e. in the break room or on a notice board.
